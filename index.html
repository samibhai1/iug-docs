$IUG documentation

$IUG is a token on Arc that pays the people holding it. Every trade in its Uniswap pool is charged a 5% fee, that fee is turned into USDC, and 80% of it is sent out to holders every 15 minutes. You are paid in USDC for holding — not in more of the token, and not on a promise to pay later.

There is no team token allocation, no owner who can change the fee or the split, and no lock-up on your side. The entire supply sits in a locked liquidity pool, the fee split is fixed in the contract, and rewards are pushed to your wallet on their own.

If you hold $IUG

Start with How the loop works to see where your USDC comes from, then Hold & earn for how to buy and what qualifies you for a round. Tokenomics & guarantees covers what is fixed forever and why, and Safety & FAQ answers the questions worth asking before you buy anything.

If you want to earn from posting

The 10% growth share funds raid rewards — USDC paid to people who post about $IUG and pass a quality gate. Raiding explains how to register, what counts, and how posts are scored.

The short version

  • Supply: 1,000,000,000 $IUG, all of it in the locked pool at launch
  • Reward: 80% of the 5% trade fee, in USDC, every 15 minutes
  • To qualify: hold at least $25 of $IUG at the snapshot — no hold-time requirement
  • Max wallet: 2% of supply, permanently
  • Owner: the token has none, so nothing about it can be changed after launch. The fee manager and reward distributor are owned by a multisig that cannot change the fee, the split or the max wallet (see Safety)
  • Chain: Arc (chain 5042)
