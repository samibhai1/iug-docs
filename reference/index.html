Owner powers over the token

None. The token contract has no owner and no privileged functions — no pause, no blacklist, no mint, no fee change, no max-wallet change. Supply, the 5% fee, the 80/10/10 split, and the 2% cap are fixed in code and cannot be altered by anyone.

The reward keeper

Distribution is run by a keeper, authorised by a multisig. The multisig cannot change the token, the supply, the split, or the cap — those are not its to change. What it controls is the payout machinery: which address acts as keeper, and it can trigger a distribution itself.

The bound on that is the standing pot — fees collected since the last payout, roughly one 15-minute cycle. In the worst case a compromised multisig could misdirect that much, capped further per address per round. It cannot reach fees already paid to holders, the locked liquidity, or the tokens in your wallet.
